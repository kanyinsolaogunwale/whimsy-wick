How to Play

Move

Keyboard: Space / ↑ / W to hop (tap again in mid-air for a double hop) · Esc to pause. Ghee runs on their own, so hopping is all there is!

Touch: tap to hop · hold for a higher hop · tap again mid-air to double hop

Collect

Coins line the counter. Bank them for keeps.

Gems hide up high and are worth a detour. In the shop, each gem tops up 20 coins.

Watch out

  • Flour: soft, but too dusty to walk through. Hop it.
  • Rolling pins: they patrol. Time your hop.
  • Butter & milk: slippery. Ghee glides through, so hop over what hides ahead.
  • Jam: sticky. It slows you down.
  • Little fans: they hold you to a crawl. Hop over the breeze.
