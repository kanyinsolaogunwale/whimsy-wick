How to Play

Move

Keyboard: Space / ↑ / W hops · tap again mid-air for a double hop · Esc pauses.

Touch: tap = hop · hold = higher hop · tap again mid-air to double.

Collect

Coins line the counter, bank them for keeps.

Gems hide up high, each spends like 20 coins in the shop.

Cook & Learn

Gather real ingredients as you run, they bank in the pantry.

Open the Whimsical Kitchen from the menu, pick a recipe, see how it's made, then cook it.

Watch out

  • Red marks: flour and pins crash Ghee on touch, hop them, they are never steps.
  • Flour: too dusty, hop it.
  • Pins: they patrol, time your hop.
  • Butter & milk: slippery, hop early.
  • Jam: sticky, it slows you.
  • Fans: a slowing breeze, hop over.
